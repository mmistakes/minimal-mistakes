Formatting is communication. Readers form an opinion of your care before they understand your logic.

## Vertical

- Related concepts stay close; unrelated ones are separated
- Dependents follow dependencies when it aids reading
- Concepts that change together appear together
- File size should stay scannable; huge files hide structure

## Horizontal

- Indentation conveys hierarchy
- Avoid packing too much onto one line
- Align only when it truly aids comparison — consistency beats clever columns

Team style beats personal preference. Automate with a formatter so reviews discuss design, not spaces.
