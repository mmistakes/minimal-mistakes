Hello! I'm Junbo Koh, an elementary school teacher with the Seoul Metropolitan Office of Education. </p>

<p>
My research focuses on <strong>human-centered AI in education</strong> with a theory-driven approach to addressing real-world educational challenges. I'm particularly interested in <strong>how AI systems can foster learner agency</strong> so that learners can chart their own learning paths and take ownership of their lives.
</p>

<p>
I'm currently exploring <strong>how agentic AI can support professionals' agency</strong> from a human-computer interaction (HCI) perspective, and <strong>how large language models can support collaborative learning</strong> using natural language processing (NLP) techniques.
</p>
